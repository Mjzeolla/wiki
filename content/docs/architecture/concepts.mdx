This section owns ideas whose meaning survives a change of language, framework, or platform. Keep
technology-specific mechanics with their narrower owner and link here for the shared mental model.

## Useful cross-cutting concepts

| Concept                     | Question it answers                                               |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Boundaries and ownership    | Which component owns this rule, state, and lifecycle?             |
| Contracts and compatibility | What may consumers rely on, and how can it evolve safely?         |
| State and transitions       | What states are valid, and which operations move between them?    |
| Identity and idempotency    | How are repeated or ambiguous operations recognized safely?       |
| Time and ordering           | Which ordering guarantees exist across concurrency or retries?    |
| Caching and freshness       | Who owns the source value, and how stale may a copy become?       |
| Failure domains             | Which faults can occur independently, and what survives each one? |
| Backpressure                | How does a producer respond when a consumer cannot keep up?       |
| Observability               | What evidence explains whether the system is behaving correctly?  |

When adding a concept page, define the invariant or model first, include a counterexample or common
misunderstanding, and connect it to concrete language or platform pages. Avoid a generic page when
the concept has materially different semantics in one specific system.
