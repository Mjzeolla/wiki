Learn where tasks run, how roles execute, and what repeat runs and previews can prove.

- [Controller and connections](/docs/infrastructure/ansible/concepts/controller-and-connections): Execution location, SSH identity, privilege escalation, and module runtime.
- [Plays, tasks, and roles](/docs/infrastructure/ansible/concepts/plays-tasks-and-roles): Ordered execution and reusable explicit interfaces.
- [Templates and handlers](/docs/infrastructure/ansible/concepts/templates-and-handlers): Rendered files, change notification, and service behavior.
- [Idempotency and check mode](/docs/infrastructure/ansible/concepts/idempotency-and-check-mode): Prediction limits, repeat-run evidence, and independent verification.

- [Facts and registered results](/docs/infrastructure/ansible/concepts/facts-and-registered-results): Host observations, task responses, and value ownership.

- [Conditions and loops](/docs/infrastructure/ansible/concepts/conditions-and-loops): Conditional work, iteration, and bounded polling.

- [Change reporting and failure](/docs/infrastructure/ansible/concepts/change-and-failure): Meaningful outcomes, assertions, and recovery.
