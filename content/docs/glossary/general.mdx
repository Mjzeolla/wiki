Use this page only for terms that apply across technical domains. Prefer a narrower glossary when
the domain changes the meaning.

**Canonical source**
: The single maintained location treated as authoritative for a fact or explanation. Other pages
link to it instead of copying content that can drift.

**Constraint**
: A condition a valid solution must satisfy. Constraints differ from preferences, which may be traded
off when they conflict.

**Decision**
: A selected course of action with enough context and consequences for another person to understand
what was chosen and why.

**Evidence**
: An observation, measurement, source, or reproducible result that supports or challenges a claim.
Confidence should reflect the quality and scope of the evidence.

**Owner**
: The person, team, or system responsible for a lifecycle and authorized to change its contract.
Ownership includes maintenance and decision responsibility, not merely authorship.

**Policy**
: A required rule or outcome. A procedure describes one way to satisfy a policy; keeping them distinct
allows implementation to change without silently changing the rule.

**Runbook**
: A bounded operational procedure for a known trigger, including access, safety checks, diagnosis or
action, verification, recovery, and escalation.

**Source of truth**
: The system or record whose value wins when copies disagree. A canonical documentation page and an
operational source of truth are related ideas but may be different artifacts.

**Tradeoff**
: A decision where improving one meaningful property worsens another. State both sides and the context
that makes the chosen balance appropriate.
