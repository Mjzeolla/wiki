Use emitted evidence to explain system behavior. Metrics summarize measurements, logs record events, and traces connect work across boundaries.

- [Metrics](/docs/observability/metrics): Time-series measurements and alerting.
- [Logs](/docs/observability/logs): Event collection and querying.
- [Tracing](/docs/observability/tracing): Instrumentation and cross-service causality.
- [Dashboards](/docs/observability/dashboards): Explore evidence and provision reusable views.
- [Error tracking](/docs/observability/errors): Group failures and connect them to releases.
