Operational playbooks are executable procedures for deployment, maintenance, incident mitigation,
backup, and recovery. They should be safe for a responder who understands the system but did not
write it.

## Required structure

1. **Trigger and target:** observable condition, affected environment, and component.
2. **Access and safety:** required permissions, change window, data-loss risk, and explicit stop
   conditions.
3. **Prerequisites:** backups, approvals, tools, current health, and dependent teams or systems.
4. **Procedure:** bounded, copyable steps with expected output at meaningful checkpoints.
5. **Verification:** service-level and data-integrity evidence that proves success.
6. **Rollback or recovery:** how to return to a known-safe state, including when rollback is not
   technically possible.
7. **Escalation:** owner, decision point, and evidence to include.

Avoid destructive commands with unresolved variables or broad paths. Prefer dry runs and read-only
diagnostics before mutation. A command that succeeded is not sufficient verification; confirm the
intended user-visible and data outcomes.

Link each procedure to the knowledge or architecture page explaining the system behavior. Keep
environment-specific credentials and secrets out of the wiki.
