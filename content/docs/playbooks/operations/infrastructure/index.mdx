Learn safely in isolated labs, then review real operations and explicit layer handoffs.

- [Local OpenTofu lab](/docs/playbooks/operations/infrastructure/local-opentofu-lab): A complete built-in-resource exercise with saved plans, outputs, and convergence.
- [Local Ansible lab](/docs/playbooks/operations/infrastructure/local-ansible-lab): A complete localhost role exercise with templates, verification, and a handler.
- [Review an infrastructure change](/docs/playbooks/operations/infrastructure/review-infrastructure-change): Target, evidence, review, apply, partial failures, and verification.
- [Bootstrap handoffs](/docs/playbooks/operations/infrastructure/bootstrap-handoffs): A dependency checklist from external state through application verification.
